# 11 Lessons Learned

## Technical Lessons
- **MLX / UI Development**: Building UI with low-level graphical operations introduced a steep learning curve but granted immense control over the final visual polish.
- **Packaging is Complex**: PyInstaller, asset paths, and cross-platform execution require careful planning and cannot be left to the very last minute. Testing on multiple environments early is crucial.


## Project Management Lessons
- **Ownership accelerates progress**: Having clear backend vs. frontend responsibilities minimized merge conflicts and allowed deep focus.
- **Visual Assets take time**: The workload for creating, finding, and resizing pixel-art assets was heavily underestimated initially.
- **Communication solves blockers**: Shared technical problem-solving and open discussion were the most effective ways to overcome hurdles.
