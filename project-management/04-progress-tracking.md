# 04 Progress Tracking (Planned vs Actual)

The team used an iterative approach rather than a strict predictive roadmap.

## Initial Scope vs Actual Scope
- **Initial Intention**: A solid implementation of Pac-Man using Python and MLX, meeting school criteria.
- **Actual Implementation**: Progressively expanded to include power-ups (magnet, booster), extra lives, hazards (bombs), tunnels, cheat modes, speed controls, an integrated sound system, and packaged executables for distribution.

## Evolution of Responsibilities
Initially, visual asset work was a bottleneck as Yrziqi was solely responsible for preparing images using LibreSprite. 
*Adaptation*: To accelerate progress, Amelhach began preparing and resizing visual assets when necessary to match required dimensions, showing how the team dynamically balanced the workload.

## Tracking Methodology
We relied on:
- **Feature-driven development**: Discussing a feature, deciding its frontend/backend split, implementing, integrating, and testing.
- **Git Commits**: Used for progressive tracking of milestones (e.g., adding sound logic, fixing UI bugs). (Evidence: ~550 commits reflecting continuous, iterative updates).
